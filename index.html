Understanding DR

What is diabetic retinopathy?

Diabetic retinopathy (DR) is damage to the retina's blood vessels caused by prolonged high blood sugar in people with diabetes. It progresses through stages — mild, moderate and severe non-proliferative DR, and proliferative DR — and is a leading cause of preventable blindness in working-age adults, with early detection through retinal imaging significantly improving care outcomes.

  • Often symptomless in early stages, making regular screening essential for every diabetic patient.
  • Fundus imaging can reveal microaneurysms, hemorrhages and exudates before vision changes occur.
  • Assistive AI can help clinicians prioritise which scans need urgent review.
